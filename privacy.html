Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 · 中文在下方

In short: your rooms stay on your computer. We do not collect, see or sell your data.

  • Where your data lives. Rooms, messages, manuscripts, notes and attachments are stored on the computer that runs Yaji (its background service). Nothing is uploaded to us. We run no servers that hold your data and have no user accounts on our side.
  • Your iPhone and iPad connect directly to your own computer over your network (or a VPN you choose). The connection is encrypted and pinned to your computer's certificate; the pairing token is kept in the device's Keychain.
  • AI services. When you talk to an AI, your messages and the files you share with it are sent to that AI's provider (for example Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek, Moonshot, or OpenRouter), through the tools or API keys you set up, under that provider's own terms and privacy policy.
  • Your profile. The name, email and password you enter are stored only on your computer (the password as a one-way hash), so you can sign in from your other devices.
  • Connectors (such as Gmail) are optional. Their sign-in tokens stay in your computer's Keychain; an AI uses them only in rooms where you turned them on, and asks before sending anything on your behalf.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Notifications are generated on your devices.
  • Children. Yaji is not directed to children under 13.
  • Changes. If we ever add an online service (for example, to reach your computer when you are away from home), this page will say exactly what it handles before it ships.
  • Contact. Support.

隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 30 日

一句话：你的房间留在你自己的电脑上。我们不收集、不查看、也不出售你的数据。

  • 数据在哪。房间、消息、原稿、笔记和附件都存在运行雅集的那台电脑上（它的后台服务），不会上传给我们。我们没有存放你数据的服务器，也没有我们这边的用户账号。
  • iPhone 和 iPad 通过你的网络（或你自己选择的 VPN）直接连到你的电脑。连接是加密的，并且只认你电脑的证书；配对凭证保存在设备的钥匙串里。
  • AI 服务。你和某个 AI 对话时，消息和你给它的文件会通过你自己设置的工具或 API Key 发给该 AI 的提供方（例如 Anthropic、OpenAI、Google、DeepSeek、Moonshot、OpenRouter），适用它们各自的条款和隐私政策。
  • 个人资料。你填写的名字、邮箱和密码只存在你的电脑上（密码以不可逆的哈希保存），用于从你的其他设备登录。
  • 连接器（例如 Gmail）是可选的。授权凭证保存在你电脑的钥匙串里；AI 只在你开启它的房间里使用，替你发送任何内容前都会先问你。
  • 没有统计分析、没有广告、没有追踪。通知在你的设备上生成。
  • 儿童。雅集不面向 13 岁以下儿童。
  • 变更。如果将来加入在线服务（例如出门在外时连回家里的电脑），会在上线前在本页写明它处理哪些数据。
  • 联系。见 支持 页面。
